## What the simulation *does* establish

The I2C conversation is correct and within standard-mode timing; the MCP23017 receives the right register numbers and values;
button handling gives exactly one action per press; the dice show every face once per roll; fault codes appear for the three
failure types. See [sim/README.md](sim/README.md).
